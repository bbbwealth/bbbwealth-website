A clear 36-month planning rule用36个月，把规划算清楚

Coverage target = total monthly financial commitments × 36 months.保障目标＝每月财务承担总额×36个月。

Additional cover = the target minus applicable existing critical illness lump-sum cover, with a minimum of zero. If cover exceeds this target, we show the excess separately.需要补足的保障＝保障目标减去现有适用的一次性重疾保额，最低显示0。若现有保额高于目标，超出部分会另外显示。

Include the regular payments you would still need to meet during treatment and recovery. Use monthly instalments, not the entire outstanding loan balance. Do not enter income, a medical-card annual limit or a one-off treatment bill as monthly commitments.填入治疗和恢复期间仍需承担的经常性支出。贷款应填每月供款，不是整笔未偿余额。不要把收入、医药卡年度限额或一次性治疗账单当成每月承担。

The period is fixed at three years. This simple rule does not forecast recovery duration, inflation, future changes in commitments or medical expenses. It does not deduct savings or assume income will continue. Review your individual needs and policy details before deciding on cover.这里固定按三年计算，不预测实际恢复时间、通胀、未来承担变化或医疗费。也不会扣除存款，或假设你仍有收入。决定保障前，请结合个人情况和保单条款一起评估。
